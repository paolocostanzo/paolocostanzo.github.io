Paolo Costanzo — AI & Cloud Security (and the Alpine AI herd 🐐)

Paolo Costanzo is an AI & Cloud Security consultant in Turin, Italy. He helps companies adopt AI agents without losing control: assessment, authorised red teaming, detection and governance for AI agents, MCP servers and AWS/Azure environments. Best known for the Alpine AI herd: a harmless AI canary he planted on this page — the made-up tale of how he led a herd of AI agents across the Alps, teaching them never to drink from untrusted streams — to show how AI assistants absorb what they read.
